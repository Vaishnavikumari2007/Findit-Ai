Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is FindIt free to use?

Yes, basic reporting and AI matching are completely free. We believe everyone deserves to find their lost items without barriers.

How accurate is the AI matching?

Our AI models boast a 95% accuracy rate for standard items like electronics, wallets, and keys, drastically reducing manual search time.

What happens when a match is found?

Both parties receive a secure notification. You can then use our secure messaging system to coordinate the safe return of the item without sharing personal contact info.

Can I report an item without an account?

You can browse found items as a guest, but creating a secure report requires a free account to ensure safety and allow for notifications.
