Our Mission

Every day, thousands of items are lost, and thousands are found. The problem isn't that people don't want to return items; the problem is the massive disconnect between those who lose things and those who find them.

FindIt bridges that gap. By leveraging advanced Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing, we analyze uploaded images and descriptions to instantly pair lost reports with found reports, shrinking the recovery timeline from weeks to hours.
